I already have a portfolio. It lives at [ani.arkhins.com](https://ani.arkhins.com): an anime-themed site with guest characters, scroll effects and more moving parts than some products I have shipped. I like it. But every time I wanted to write something down, the machinery got in the way, and a site you never update is just a business card with extra steps.

So this is the other one. It is a small Next.js site of mostly static pages, and it exists for two reasons: a place to write, and a place that still looks right when I have not touched it in six months.

Both carry the same work. Everything you would expect is on the [home page](/): projects with their screenshots, experience, education, certifications, and a form that actually reaches me. The anime one tells the same story with a lot more colour. This blog is where the rest goes: notes on interfaces, models, the web, and whatever else I am learning at the time.
