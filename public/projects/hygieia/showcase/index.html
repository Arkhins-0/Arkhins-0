Overview

Clinical-grade prediction, an assistant, and an audit trail. One platform.

A clinician or a curious patient can move from a heart-risk questionnaire to a dermatology image scan without changing tools. Every prediction is hashed into an append-only chain, so a result can be re-verified later and any tampering shows up as a broken link.

How a prediction is made

From a form or an image to a verified record.

INPUT → FEATURES → ENSEMBLE → CALIBRATION → RESULT → SHA-256 CHAIN

01

Guided input

Context-aware fields with real-time validation and helper text for every medical parameter. Skin lesions are uploaded as images; the other models take questionnaires and lab values.

  • Toggles and units tuned per parameter
  • Image upload with progress states
  • Clear disclaimer: a risk assessment, not a diagnosis
02

Results and history

The dashboard keeps recent analyses, per-model counts and an activity timeline, with quick cards into every diagnostic flow. Each result carries its confidence score and risk level.

  • Per-model counters
  • Risk level and confidence on every result
  • One click back into any analysis
03

Dr. Hygieia

A context-aware assistant that answers with your own analysis history in view. Multiple concurrent conversations, streaming responses and direct links into specific results.

  • Streaming answers
  • Session management
  • Deep links to analyses
04

Blockchain verification

Every record is hashed with SHA-256 and appended to a chain. Verification walks the chain, flags any tampering and gives administrators an oversight dashboard.

  • Immutable audit trail
  • Tamper detection and chain validation
  • Admin oversight

Interface

Calm, accessible, built for long sessions.

Dark and light mode, responsive by default: the same flows work on a ward tablet and on a phone in a waiting room.
