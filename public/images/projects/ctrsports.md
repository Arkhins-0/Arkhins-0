# CTR Unified — One Nation. One Championship.

## Six Sports, One Identity

CTR Unified is the parent organisation behind Chennai Turbo Riders, and this site is its front door. Where the [Chennai Turbo Riders](https://chennaiturboriders.racing) build was about a single racing team, this one had a much harder brief: put motorsport, hockey, pickleball, volleyball and cricket under one roof without the result feeling like five websites stapled together.

That constraint shaped every decision. One typographic system, one crest language, one grid — and enough visual room for each discipline to still feel like itself.

## The Journey

### Why This Project Matters to Me

Building the CTR racing site earned the trust that led to this one. Going from "team website" to "the digital identity of a multi-discipline sports collective" was a genuine step up in scope — more stakeholders, more content types, and a brand that has to hold up across a Formula 4 paddock and a cricket ground alike.

It also carries a national championship on its back. The CTR × JK Tyre FMSCI Indian National Car Racing Championship launches its inaugural 2026 season through this site, which meant the calendar, the categories and the registration path had to be unambiguous to a first-time visitor.

### Technical Challenges Overcome

- **One system, six brands**: Building a component set flexible enough for six disciplines while keeping a single, recognisable CTR identity.
- **Content that keeps moving**: A newsroom and championship bulletin that update through the season without a redeploy for every post.
- **Media weight**: Crest artwork, action photography and team imagery, all delivered without wrecking load times on a trackside mobile connection.
- **Information hierarchy**: Making a four-round, seven-category championship legible at a glance — dates, circuits, classes, entry.
- **Multi-surface routing**: A main organisational site plus a dedicated championship section at `/incrc` that reads as its own destination while staying inside the same shell.

## What Makes This Special

### A Collective, Not a Catalogue

The core idea is stated on the page itself — *"one standard of preparation, one identity, and one long-term commitment."* The design had to argue that visually: the same treatment for a hockey squad and an F4 development driver, so nothing reads as a secondary sport.

### Grassroots to Global

The site frames CTR's pathway explicitly — *"A new generation is ready to take the wheel, challenge the best, and carry India onto the global stage."* Sections are ordered to walk a visitor from the organisation, through the disciplines, into the championship.

### Live and Load-Bearing

The site is the official presence for a national championship with rounds at Kari Motor Speedway, Bren Raceway and the Madras International Circuit. It is not a brochure — teams, partners and prospective entrants use it as the source of truth.

## Technical Highlights

### Built With Pride

- **Next.js (App Router)**: Prerendered pages with incremental revalidation, so editorial content refreshes without a rebuild.
- **TypeScript**: Typed content models for teams, disciplines, rounds and news entries.
- **Tailwind CSS**: A custom CTR design system — crest-led, high contrast, motorsport-derived.
- **Optimised media**: Responsive images and lazy loading across a heavily photographic site.
- **Vercel**: Edge-cached delivery from the Mumbai region, close to the audience.
- **SEO & metadata**: Structured titles and descriptions per discipline and per championship page.

### Features I'm Most Proud Of

- **The unified sports grid**: Six disciplines presented as peers, each with its own competitive framing.
- **Championship venues and calendar**: The full 2026 round list — dates, circuits, cities — in one scannable block.
- **Championship bulletin and newsroom**: Two distinct editorial streams for official notices and general news.
- **Crest system**: Per-sport emblems that stay coherent as a family.
- **Contact and enquiry flow**: A direct line to the organisation from every page.

## The Impact

### For CTR

- **Single identity**: One address for an organisation that previously spoke through five separate team channels.
- **Championship launch platform**: The public face of the inaugural INCRC season and its FMSCI and JK Tyre partnerships.
- **Recruitment and partnerships**: A credible surface for athletes, sponsors and media to evaluate the organisation.

### For Me Personally

- **Scope**: My largest brand-systems project — a design language stretched across six sports rather than one.
- **Trust**: A repeat engagement, which is the feedback I value most.
- **Editorial architecture**: Real experience designing for content that changes weekly rather than shipping and freezing.

## Technical Implementation

### Technology Stack

- **Framework**: Next.js (App Router)
- **Language**: TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with a custom CTR theme
- **Rendering**: Prerendered pages with incremental static revalidation
- **Hosting**: Vercel, edge-cached
- **Content**: Structured, typed content models per discipline and per round

### Design Features

- **Crest-led identity**: Per-sport emblems inside a single visual family
- **Editorial rhythm**: Long-scroll sections with alternating media and statement copy
- **Responsive**: Mobile-first, built for use at venues and on the move
- **Performance**: Optimised imagery and static delivery on a media-heavy site
- **Accessibility**: Semantic structure, keyboard-navigable, high-contrast type
