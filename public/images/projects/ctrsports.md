# CTR — Indian National Car Racing Championship

## One Nation, One Championship

ctrsports.in is the official home of the CTR–JK Tyre FMSCI Indian National Car Racing Championship (INCRC). Where the earlier CTR builds were a team site and then a multi-sport organisational front door, this one is a championship portal: a live newsroom, a season schedule with a round countdown, results, drivers' and teams' standings, tickets and hospitality, videos, polls and a fan zone with its own accounts — all under one CTR identity.

The brief was simple to say and hard to do: make a first-season national championship feel established from day one, and give fans, teams and partners a single source of truth for everything that happens between Coimbatore, Bengaluru and Chennai.

## The Journey

### Why This Project Matters to Me

The Chennai Turbo Riders site earned the trust for CTR Unified, and CTR Unified earned the trust for this. Each step widened the scope; this one changed its nature. A championship portal is not a brochure — it is an editorial product that updates every race weekend, with standings that must be right, a calendar that must be unambiguous and a ticket path that has to work for someone standing at a circuit gate on a phone.

It also carries the CTR story publicly: from taking the track in 2022 to launching a national championship with JK Tyre and the FMSCI in 2026.

### Technical Challenges Overcome

- **A newsroom that leads**: The homepage is a magazine — a breaking lead story, a grid of secondary features, a list of venue guides and explainers — rather than a hero-and-scroll landing page.
- **Live season state**: A ticker across the top of every page shows the current round, session and a countdown ("Round 1 – Coimbatore · ISC – Practice · 6d 09h 13m"), so the site always knows where the championship is.
- **Standings and results**: Drivers' and teams' tables with points, wins and team affiliations that update round by round.
- **Fan accounts**: Sign in / register, predictions, polls, favourites and a newsletter — the first CTR property with a logged-in experience.
- **Hospitality as product**: Four ticket tiers explained clearly enough that a fan can choose between the Grand Stand and the CTR Executive Lounge without calling anyone.
- **Consent done properly**: A cookie layer that only asks about embedded YouTube video — "there is no advertising or tracking on this site."

## What Makes This Special

### Championship-First Information Architecture

The primary navigation is the championship itself: Latest, Schedule, Results, Standings, Drivers, Teams, Tickets. Everything about the organisation lives behind that, in "The World of CTR" and the partners page. A visitor never has to work out what CTR is before finding out who is leading.

### Editorial Rhythm

Launch news, venue guides for each circuit, a seven-categories explainer and the calendar reveal are all first-class content, not blog afterthoughts. The design borrows from motorsport broadcast graphics — yellow-on-black, a signature wave rule, condensed type — so it reads like a championship, not a startup.

### Built to Be the Source of Truth

Teams, partners, media and entrants use the site as the canonical record for dates, sessions, results and points. That is a different standard of correctness from a marketing site, and the content model was designed around it.

## Technical Highlights

### Built With Pride

- **Next.js (App Router)**: Prerendered pages with incremental revalidation, so news, results and standings refresh without a rebuild.
- **TypeScript**: Typed content models for rounds, sessions, categories, drivers, teams, articles and ticket tiers.
- **Tailwind CSS**: A custom CTR championship theme — yellow signal colour, black chrome, condensed display type.
- **Accounts and engagement**: Authenticated fan zone with polls, predictions, favourites and newsletter subscription.
- **Optimised media**: Responsive imagery and lazy-loaded embeds across a photo-heavy newsroom.
- **Edge delivery**: Cached from the Mumbai region, close to the audience.

### Features I'm Most Proud Of

- **The round ticker**: Round, session and live countdown on every page.
- **Standings tables**: Drivers' and teams' championships with points and wins at a glance.
- **Tickets & hospitality**: Grand Stand, Riders Terrace, Turbo Deck and CTR Executive Lounge, each with its inclusions spelled out.
- **Venue guides**: Kari Motor Speedway, Bren Raceway and Madras International Circuit as editorial destinations.
- **The Fan Zone**: Predictions, polls, favourites and a newsletter behind a free registration.
